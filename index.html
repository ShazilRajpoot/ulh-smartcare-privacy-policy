1Introduction

Welcome to ULH SmartCare ("the App"), developed by REDSOL Technologies on behalf of the University of Lahore Hospital ("the Hospital," "we," "us," or "our").

ULH SmartCare is a patient-facing mobile application that gives registered patients of the University of Lahore Hospital secure, read-only access to their own hospital records, and allows them to book appointments. All information shown in the App comes directly from the Hospital's Hospital Management Information System (HMIS), where it was recorded during the patient's registration and treatment at the Hospital.

The App does not ask you to provide new personal information, and it does not create, edit, or independently store your medical records. Its only purposes are to display your existing records and to let you book appointments.

By installing or using ULH SmartCare, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to registered patients of the University of Lahore Hospital who use ULH SmartCare on Android or iOS devices, and to parents or legal guardians who access the account of a minor patient as described in Section 9.

It does not apply to Hospital staff accounts, which are governed by a separate internal data-handling policy.

3Information the App uses

3.1 Information already held by the Hospital

All of your personal and medical information is already held in the Hospital's HMIS from your registration and visits. The App retrieves and displays this information; it does not collect it from you. This may include:

  • Personal details: full name, CNIC, phone number, email address, home address, date of birth, gender
  • Medical Record Number (MRN) and blood group
  • Appointment history (doctors, specialties, dates, queue positions)
  • Laboratory test orders, results, and reference ranges
  • Radiology orders and reports
  • Prescriptions and dispensing history
  • Billing records (encounter numbers, amounts, payment status)
  • Inpatient admission details (ward, bed, attending physician, diagnosis), when applicable
  • Clinical notes and discharge summaries, when made available by the Hospital

The Hospital remains the sole custodian of all these records. The App only displays them.

3.2 Information you enter in the App

The App asks you to enter information in only two situations:

  • Login: your CNIC and/or phone number and the one-time password (OTP) sent to you. These are sent to the HMIS solely to verify that you are an existing registered patient. They are matched against records the Hospital already holds and are not used for any other purpose.
  • Appointment booking: your selected doctor or specialty, date, and time slot. This booking request is sent directly to the HMIS and becomes part of your hospital appointment record.

The App does not offer any other way to submit, upload, or change information.

3.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect precise or approximate location data; device contacts, call logs, or SMS messages; photos, camera, or microphone input; advertising identifiers or tracking data; social media information; or behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking data.

4How we use information

Information is used only to:

  • Verify your identity against existing HMIS records before granting access.
  • Display your records, including appointments, lab results, radiology reports, prescriptions, billing, and admission information, exactly as they exist in the HMIS.
  • Book appointments by sending your booking request to the HMIS.
  • Manage your session so you do not have to log in on every visit.
  • Show in-app notifications about ready lab results, upcoming appointments, and similar updates from the HMIS.
  • Remember your App settings on your device.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to your care at the University of Lahore Hospital.

5You see only your own records

Each patient can view only their own records. After login, the HMIS returns data linked exclusively to the verified patient's account (MRN). The App cannot be used to search for, view, or access any other patient's information.

Every data request is authorized on the server side using your session token, so records belonging to other patients are never sent to your device.

6How information is stored

6.1 On the Hospital's servers

All medical records, personal details, billing data, appointment records, and lab results are stored on the University of Lahore Hospital's HMIS servers. The App fetches this data on demand. REDSOL Technologies does not operate separate servers that store your personal or medical information.

6.2 On your device

Locally stored data (Section 3.3) is kept within the App's private storage area and is not accessible to other apps.

6.3 Retention

  • Sessions expire automatically after 30 days, after which you must log in again.
  • Logging out removes your session token from the device.
  • Cached PDFs can be cleared any time via Settings → Data & Storage → Clear Cache, and are removed when the App is uninstalled.
  • Medical records are retained on the HMIS according to the Hospital's records retention policy and applicable Pakistani healthcare regulations.

8Security

  • Encrypted transmission: all communication between the App and the HMIS uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • Token-based authentication: access is managed through a time-limited session token rather than your raw credentials.
  • Server-side access control: each request is checked so that only your own records are returned.
  • Sandboxed local storage: on-device data is confined to the App's private storage.
  • Automatic session expiry after 30 days.
  • Optional biometric lock (fingerprint or Face ID) via Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Auto-lock timer requiring re-authentication after inactivity.

No digital system is completely immune to security risks. Please keep your device secure and never share your CNIC, phone, or OTP with anyone.

If you believe your account has been accessed without permission, contact the Hospital's Front Desk or IT Help Desk immediately.

9Children's privacy

The Hospital may hold records for minor patients. A minor's account may only be accessed by their parent or legal guardian as registered with the Hospital. The App does not collect information from children; all minor patient records originate from the Hospital's registration process.

If you believe a minor's information has been accessed inappropriately, please contact us using the details in Section 14.

10Your rights

  • Access: you may view your own records in the App at any time, subject to what the HMIS makes available.
  • Correction: the App is read-only. To correct any personal or clinical information, please contact the Hospital directly (Front Desk or Patient Services). Corrections made in the HMIS will be reflected in the App automatically.
  • Deleting App data from your device: you can clear cached data, log out, or uninstall the App at any time.
  • Account and record deletion: requests to deactivate your App access or to delete records held in the HMIS must be made directly to the Hospital and are subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Notifications: you may turn off any notification category via Settings → Notifications.
